# Terms of Service

Dee IdleClans Helper ("the bot") is a free Discord bot that reports Idle Clans market data. It is
run by an individual developer ("we"). It is not affiliated with Idle Clans or with Discord.

Last updated: 2026-11-25.

## The service

Every number the bot prints is a market fact or arithmetic over market facts, read from the game's
public API. It gives no profit advice and no game formula. Prices may be stale, missing or wrong:
the game's market moves, and a quote is a snapshot, not a promise.

## Acceptable use

- Do not try to overload, scrape or bypass the bot's rate limits.
- Do not resell access to the bot.
- Follow Discord's Terms of Service and the rules of the server you are in.
- Do not use the bot where it would break the rules of the Idle Clans community.

We may block a user or a server that abuses the service.
